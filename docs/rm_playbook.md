# Relationship Manager Playbook

## Start of day

1. Review the capacity-allocated worklist, control banner and overdue tasks.
2. Prioritise High before Medium, but do not bypass customer context.
3. Read product need, propensity, uplift, economics and all three reason codes.
4. Check authoritative KYC, AML, credit and CRM systems for changes after the analytical cutoff.

## Conversation preparation

- Identify the business observation that created the need signal.
- Prepare one open discovery question; do not lead with a product pitch.
- Review existing products to avoid duplication or channel conflict.
- Check prior contact outcome and preferred channel.
- For FX, document exposure and risk objective; never frame speculation as hedging.

## Permitted dispositions

| Disposition | When to use |
|---|---|
| Contacted - need confirmed | Customer confirms relevant need; route to authorised process |
| Contacted - no current need | Evidence is stale or customer declines; retain outcome |
| Deferred | Timing is inappropriate; set a reasoned follow-up date |
| Suppressed after review | New KYC, AML, credit, permission or suitability issue |
| Data correction required | Source evidence is incomplete or wrong |

## Quality standard

Record supporting and contradictory evidence, customer need, channel, outcome, next step and owner.
Do not overwrite a suppression, approve a product or represent the analytical score as a decision.

## Manager review

Weekly: capacity, SLA, conversion, override reasons, suppressions and customer complaints. Monthly:
realized economics, model monitoring, treatment/control evidence and conduct diagnostics.
