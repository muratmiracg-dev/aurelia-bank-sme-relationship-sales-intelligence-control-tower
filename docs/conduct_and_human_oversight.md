# Conduct Governance and Human Oversight

## Governing principle

The best commercial score is not necessarily the right customer conversation. Consent, current
customer context, eligibility, risk, suitability and human judgment are independent requirements.

## Pre-allocation gates

- Contact permission must be present.
- Beneficial-owner information must be complete.
- KYC cannot be materially overdue.
- High-priority AML review suppresses the opportunity.
- Credit products require PD and arrears inside sales policy and current financials.
- FX risk-management conversations require documented exposure.

## Human workflow

1. Review need evidence, model probability, uplift and economics.
2. Confirm current KYC, AML, credit and product eligibility in authoritative systems.
3. Review contradictory evidence and recent customer interactions.
4. Decide whether a conversation is appropriate; record contact, defer or decline.
5. If contacted, understand needs before discussing a product.
6. Route credit, pricing, suitability and execution to authorised processes.

## Customer rights and privacy

The portfolio contains no real personal data. A live system would require a documented processing
basis, purpose limitation, minimisation, retention, access controls, data-subject processes and
specialist legal/privacy review. KVKK Article 11 includes the right to object where analysis by
exclusively automated systems produces an adverse result. This project therefore prohibits an
automated binding outcome.

Reference: [KVKK data-subject rights](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/6938/Kurumumuza-Yapilan-Sikayetlerin-Usul-Sartlarina-Iliskin-Kamuoyu-Duyurusu).

## Product governance

The framework follows the principle that products should reach an identified target market and
meet customer interests, objectives and characteristics. It does not claim EBA or Turkish
regulatory conformity.

Reference: [EBA product oversight and governance](https://www.eba.europa.eu/activities/single-rulebook/regulatory-activities/consumer-protection/guidelines-product-oversight-and-governance-arrangements-retail-banking-products).

## Non-negotiable controls

- No automated sale or acceptance.
- No bypass of KYC, AML, credit or suitability processes.
- No speculative FX prompt.
- No contact without permission and authorised review.
- No use of region or SME size to exclude customers; they are monitoring dimensions only.
