# Product Conversation Catalog

The engine prioritises conversations, not products sold. Product owners, Credit, Treasury,
Operations, Compliance and the authorised relationship manager remain responsible for eligibility,
terms, suitability and execution.

## Working Capital Loan

- Evidence: liquidity gap, existing loan balance, turnover and PD.
- Economics: utilized exposure, margin, fee, FTP, expected loss and capital charge.
- Gates: PD, arrears, current financials, KYC and AML.
- Conversation: understand cash-conversion cycle and seasonal funding need.

## POS & Merchant Acquiring

- Evidence: merchant/POS share, digital activity and scale.
- Economics: annual settlement volume and acquiring margin.
- Gate: consent, KYC/AML and actual merchant need.

## Business Credit Card

- Evidence: digital engagement, employee base, scale and loan use.
- Economics: utilization, interchange/fee proxy, FTP, expected loss and capital.
- Gates: credit policy and authorised limit decision.

## Payroll Services

- Evidence: employee count and recurring payroll outflows.
- Economics: annual payroll flow and service-fee proxy.
- Boundary: operational feasibility and customer confirmation.

## Cash Management

- Evidence: payments, collections, total flows and digital engagement.
- Economics: managed flow and fee/margin assumptions.
- Boundary: process discovery before solution design.

## Trade Finance

- Evidence: importer/exporter flags, trade and FX flows.
- Economics: utilized trade exposure, fee, expected loss and capital charge.
- Gates: credit, document, sanctions and compliance review remain external.

## Term Deposit

- Evidence: surplus average deposits and stable inflows.
- Economics: illustrative internal funding benefit less service cost.
- Boundary: liquidity horizon, rate approval and customer preference.

## FX Risk Management

- Evidence: documented FX inflow/outflow and cross-border activity.
- Economics: hedged-notional and spread/fee proxy.
- Gates: documented need, product suitability, Treasury approval and human review.
- Explicit exclusion: speculative trading recommendations.
