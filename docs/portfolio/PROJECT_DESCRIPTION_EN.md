# Portfolio Description

## Short version

Built an end-to-end SME relationship-banking and sales intelligence platform for a fictional bank,
combining Customer 360, eight-product propensity, treatment/control uplift, risk-adjusted
profitability, conduct gates, next-best-conversation and relationship-manager capacity. The
solution includes Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL/SQLite, Power BI, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, CI and 39
tests with a 90% coverage gate.

## CV version

- Engineered a deterministic 3,200-customer SME relationship dataset and Customer 360 spanning
  cash flow, products, CRM, credit risk, KYC and AML context.
- Developed a temporally validated logistic champion (ROC AUC 0.715, PR AUC 0.495, 2.07x
  top-decile lift) plus nonlinear challenger and treatment/control uplift diagnostics.
- Integrated need, uplift, FTP-style funding, expected loss, capital charge and conduct gates into
  661 human-reviewed next-best conversations with TRY 16.74m illustrative expected incremental
  profit.
- Delivered API, SQL, Power BI, formula-driven Excel, executive deck/report, CI, CodeQL,
  reproducibility evidence and 97%+ local coverage.

## LinkedIn version

I completed the fourth module in the Aurelia Bank analytics portfolio: an SME Relationship & Sales
Intelligence Control Tower. Instead of ranking customers only by response probability, the platform
combines customer need, incremental contact uplift, risk-adjusted economics, KYC/AML/credit gates
and relationship-manager capacity. Every output remains human-led and the project intentionally
surfaces a KYC backlog breach rather than hiding it. The repository contains the complete Python,
API, SQL, Power BI, Excel, presentation, PDF, governance and testing stack.
